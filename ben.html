About Bernard & Family

Building trust, one home at a time.

Founded by Bernard Mugisha and his family, Bernard & Family Real Estate has been a cornerstone in the Ugandan property market for over a decade. With a deep-rooted passion for honest service and community development, we specialize in residential and commercial real estate across Mbarara, Kakoba, Kabale and beyond.

What makes us different? We treat every client like family. From first-time buyers to seasoned investors, we offer personalized guidance, transparent negotiations, and unmatched local expertise. Our portfolio includes luxurious estates, affordable housing solutions, and land acquisition services.

🌟 Our mission: To create lasting value and joyful homes while empowering families through smart property decisions.

🤝 Core values: Integrity, excellence, warmth, and community impact.
